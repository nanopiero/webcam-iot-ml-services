Choice: checkpoint recovery and backfill have separate responsibilities

Reference: ~/doc/ML_services_architecture/architecture:2376
Section: Backfill and replay scheduling.

S3 checkpoint recovery and backfill have separate responsibilities. Restarting
from a valid S3 checkpoint restores a coherent operational state, even when
some earlier images were skipped. A coherent state need not incorporate every
available image. Checkpoint recovery is not responsible for filling these
gaps; backfill occurs afterwards.

The earlier choice to exclude backfill scene assignments from operational
history is superseded by "Correction: backfill revises scene history while
preserving produced values" in corrections.txt.

Backfill rebuilds the affected stream's scene history chronologically, fills
missing outputs, and updates existing scene indices while preserving existing
valid raw and post-processed values. Subsequent C+Q uses the revised scene
history, and live processing resumes from the rebuilt final scene state.
Backfill does not republish existing observations or advance latest-output
pointers. See corrections.txt for the complete persistence and provenance rules.


Choice: no additional acquisition recovery mechanism for the pilot

Reference: ~/doc/ML_services_architecture/architecture:1869
Section: Idempotency, ordering anomalies and retries.

An acquisition crash may lose in-flight work or lead to notifications or jobs
being resent. This behavior is accepted for the pilot; no additional durable
acquisition recovery or reconciliation mechanism is required.

Remove the suggestion to skip MQTT notifications solely because msg.dup=1.
The MQTT DUP flag does not establish that the application has already handled
the notification, so it must not be used alone as a reason to discard it.
